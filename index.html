Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about admissions, fees, transport, and curriculum.

For Nursery, the child must be at least 3 years of age as of March 31 of the academic session year. For Class 1, the child must be at least 5.5 to 6 years of age. Age relaxations can be discussed with the administration team.

Required documents include: (1) Self-attested copy of Birth Certificate, (2) Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous recognized school (for Class 2 and above), (3) Previous grade Marksheet/Report Card, (4) Aadhaar Card of student & parent, and (5) 4 recent passport-size photographs of the student.

Fees can be paid monthly (by the 10th of each month) or quarterly in four easy installments: Quarter 1 (April), Quarter 2 (July), Quarter 3 (October), and Quarter 4 (January). Payments can be made via UPI, NetBanking, or directly at the school accounts counter.
